Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about VividP

What makes VividP different from other IDP platforms? +
VividP is the first truly AI-native IDP platform, combining zero-trust security, WebGPU-accelerated visualization, and natural language queries in one unified experience. Our eBPF-based observability provides unprecedented visibility into your infrastructure.
How does the AI-powered analytics work? +
Our AI engine analyzes your infrastructure patterns, predicts resource needs, detects anomalies in real-time, and provides actionable insights to optimize performance and reduce costs. It's powered by Google's Vertex AI platform.
Can VividP integrate with our existing tools? +
Yes, VividP integrates seamlessly with popular DevOps tools including Kubernetes, Docker, Jenkins, GitHub Actions, Prometheus, Grafana, and many more. Our API-first architecture makes custom integrations straightforward.
What security standards does VividP meet? +
VividP is built with security-first principles and meets SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and GDPR compliance standards. Our zero-trust architecture ensures continuous verification and granular access controls.
How does the natural language query feature work? +
Simply type or speak your query in natural language (e.g., "Show me deployment latency trends for the last 30 days"), and our AI will generate the appropriate visualizations and insights instantly.
What deployment options are available? +
VividP offers flexible deployment options including our managed SaaS platform, self-hosted on your infrastructure, or hybrid deployments. Enterprise customers can choose the deployment model that best fits their security requirements.
